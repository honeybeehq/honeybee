accounts.proactiveOwnerWaitMs defaults 90000, accounts.proactiveOwnerCooldownMs defaults 900000. Both accept finite values >=1000ms. The short minimum permits deliberately fast fixture or operator settings; production owner round trips may need longer. During cooldown the durable cancelled row makes proactiveTargets return an empty, certain comparison. Fresh observations and daemon restarts cannot bypass it. Once the cooldown passes, a later opening may attempt relative admission again. With an absent owner a busy bee can pay one 90s wait per cooldown cycle. The provider-limit recovery still handles actual exhaustion.

Expiry does not prove owner death: an owner that reported capacity or credential waiting can also exceed the deadline. owner_unavailable means the proactive owner did not complete a claim in time.
